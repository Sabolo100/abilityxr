OpenClaw Eszter

OpenClaw Eszter is a private AI-powered automation assistant used by a limited number of authorized users.

The service helps its users perform everyday productivity tasks such as processing email instructions, accessing documents, retrieving information and working with files stored in connected Google services.

Depending on the permissions explicitly granted by the user, OpenClaw Eszter may access:

  • Gmail messages and related metadata
  • Google Drive files and folders
  • Google Docs
  • Google Sheets

Access to Google services is used only to perform tasks requested by authorized users.

OpenClaw Eszter is not a public consumer service. Access is restricted to explicitly authorized accounts.
